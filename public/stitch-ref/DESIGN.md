## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a fresh, appetizing, and technologically streamlined food curation experience. Tailored for corporate planners, event hosts, and discerning culinary enthusiasts, it bridges modern algorithmic intelligence with farm-to-table vitality. 

The aesthetic style merges **Warm Organic Minimalism** with crisp **Modern Utility**:
- **Clarity over Clutter**: Generous breathing room, light mint-tinted surfaces, and stark charcoal typography.
- **Vibrant Hospitality**: Inviting, rounded silhouettes and organic soft-lime accents convey health, sustainability, and prompt service.
- **Smart Precision**: Compact stat badges, neatly structured nutritional breakdowns, and linear iconography provide reliable analytical rigor without sacrificing culinary delight.

## Colors

The color palette centers on lush, natural greens complemented by anchored dark slate neutrals and delicate culinary tints.

- **Primary (`#82C46C`)**: The core brand accent denoting freshness, botanical vitality, active confirmations, key conversion buttons, and prominent highlight blocks.
- **Secondary (`#2B2F33`)**: Deep charcoal used for primary headings, high-contrast action buttons, and active status tags.
- **Tertiary (`#F87171`)**: Coral salmon applied for critical calorie counts, dietary warnings, urgent actions, and favorites.
- **Accent Warmth (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber yellow used exclusively for review stars and chef awards.
- **Tonal Mint Backgrounds (`#EEF7EA`, `#F4FAF0`)**: Subtle green tinted containers reserved for card highlights, counter tiles, and feature panels.
- **Neutral Scales**: `#6D7278` handles secondary text and metadata; `#F8FAF8` delivers an airy, tinted canvas foundation paired with pure `#FFFFFF` cards.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across headlines, body copy, and UI controls to preserve geometric purity and a friendly, accessible cadence.

- **Headlines**: Rendered in heavy weights (700 to 800) with slight negative tracking to achieve compact, punchy editorial titles. Hand-drawn highlight loops or primary color accents can be paired with key keywords.
- **Subheadings & Section Intros**: Styled with muted tones (`#6D7278`) and steady line heights to maintain effortless scannability before dense culinary menus.
- **Nutritional Labels & Pricing**: Set with precise, medium-to-bold metrics for rapid cognitive intake during order planning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid structure constrained to a maximum content container of `1280px`. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) page margins. Catering menus and feature blocks expand naturally into 4 or 3-column rows.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Grids collapse into 2-column card structures.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Cards collapse into horizontal carousels or single-column stacks with edge-to-edge imagery.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Inter-section gaps systematically utilize `5rem` to `7rem` on desktop and `3rem` on mobile, maintaining an airy, uncluttered presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on clean surface layering, crisp white cards, and ultra-diffuse ambient shadows rather than sharp border drops:

- **Canvas Base**: Soft ambient ground (`#F8FAF8`).
- **Elevated Food & Review Cards**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces layered above canvas using a soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(43, 47, 51, 0.05)`.
- **Soft Highlight Containers**: Filled flat tiles (`#EEF7EA` or `#82C46C` at 15% opacity) with no shadows, serving as background trays for metrics, stats, and workflow steps.
- **Interactive Modals & Overlays**: Floating menus and dropdowns leverage `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(43, 47, 51, 0.10)`.
- **Borders**: Avoid harsh divider strokes; utilize delicate tints (`rgba(109, 114, 120, 0.12)`) only when necessary to define input forms or search wrappers.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, playful, and ultra-friendly geometries:

- **Corner Radii**: Cards, feature boxes, and pricing sheets employ generous curvatures ranging from `1.5rem` (24px) to `2rem` (32px).
- **Controls & Chips**: Buttons, search bars, pagination controls, and micro-badges are strictly full pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Media Containers**: Food photography utilizes perfect circles (`rounded-full`) or smooth rounded rectangles (`rounded-3xl`), mimicking plates, platters, and organic serving surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Full-pill background of `#82C46C` with white text (`#FFFFFF`) or `#2B2F33` bold label, slight scale animation on hover (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Contrast Action**: Deep charcoal (`#2B2F33`) pill with white text, providing visual anchor next to primary buttons.
- **Icon / Pagination Buttons**: Circular discs (`40px x 40px`) in filled mint (`#EEF7EA`) or dark charcoal (`#2B2F33`) containing minimalist linear arrows.
- **Counter / Add Button**: Circular primary green badge (`#82C46C`) centered with a white `+` icon positioned alongside dish pricing.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutritional / Meta Badges**: Soft gray or mint micro-pills containing metadata such as calorie count (`🔥 150 Kcal`) or customer ratings (`⭐ 4.8`).
- **Step Indicators**: Small circular badges (`24px x 24px`) in dark charcoal (`#2B2F33`) with white numeric values denoting flow step numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`).

### Cards & Packaging Blocks
- **Dish Menu Card**: Clean white vertical card with high-resolution circular dish image overlapping the header, title in charcoal, ratings row, price in bold, and a green plus-action button pinned to the lower right.
- **Workflow & Stat Card**: Uniform colored panels in soft mint (`#82C46C` tint) with oversized bold metric typography (`10K+`, `09+`), accompanied by a centered descriptive label.
- **Pricing Packages**: Dual comparison cards; standard package in white, recommended package highlighted in filled light mint (`#EEF7EA`) with full-width primary CTA pill.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Bar**: Full pill container with light tinted border (`#E5E7EB`), interior search icon, subtle placeholder text, and an embedded right-aligned green submit pill button.
- **Newsletter Box**: Muted background pill (`#F3F4F6`) with embedded icon, clean text field, and adjacent green action pill.
